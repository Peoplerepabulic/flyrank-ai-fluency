# Decide Once: Identity Kit

**Track:** General AI Fluency · **Week:** 3 · **Estimated:** 2h

One page. Every choice below is final until the portfolio ships — no more
font-shopping mid-build.

## Type

- **Headings:** Space Grotesk (Google Fonts, free, OFL) — geometric, confident,
  reads well at hero sizes.
- **Body:** Inter (Google Fonts, free, OFL) — neutral workhorse, excellent at
  small sizes and in long case-study text.
- Two fonts total. Nothing else.

## Color

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Text | `#1A1A1A` | Near-black body text |
| Background | `#FAFAF8` | Near-white page background |
| Primary | `#2563EB` | Links, key accents, the logo dot |
| Emphasis (max 1) | `#B45309` | Sparingly: the single "hire me" CTA |

Four colors, one accent. Muted enough that screenshots of real work stay the
loudest thing on the page.

## Logo / favicon

`logo.svg` — a rounded near-black square, monospace "QL" in near-white, one
blue dot. Doubles as the favicon (SVG favicons work in all modern browsers;
falls back to the "QL" initial). No gradients, no tagline, legible at 16px.

## Style note

Space Grotesk headlines over Inter body, on warm near-white with near-black
text; blue only where something is clickable, amber only where I'm asking for
something. Mood: **a well-organized workbench — everything visible, nothing
shouting.**
